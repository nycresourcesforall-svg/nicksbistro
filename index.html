Welcome to Our Table

A Neighborhood Greek Bistro Built on Passion & Family

Founded by long-time friends and restaurateurs Mario and Alfred, Nick’s Bistro was created with a clear vision: to bring authentic Greek-American cooking into an elegant, warm, and contemporary neighborhood setting right here in Forest Hills.

Whether you are stopping by for lunch, cozying up for a candlelit seafood dinner with fine Greek wines, or celebrating a family milestone in our private party space, our goal is to treat you like family from the moment you step through our doors.
